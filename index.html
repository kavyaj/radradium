About

Radradium is run by Kavya Jahagirdar, a founder who builds for the internet from Singapore.

Resumey.Pro came first, back in 2019. In 2021 she quit her 9-to-5 to build on the internet full time, and the table has been filling up ever since.

Why "Rad Radium"?

It started as a pun. We wanted to build rad things, one element at a time, and Rad Radium made it past the shortlist.

Radium is element 88, it glows in the dark, and Rad Radium shortens to RR. The logo was right there!
